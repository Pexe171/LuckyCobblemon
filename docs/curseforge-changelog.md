## Lucky Cobblemon: Fortune Blocks 0.6.0

- Added Rare, Legendary and Cursed Lucky Blocks with unique base luck, recipes and visuals.
- Added six supply events: apricorn harvest, berry picnic, evolution stones, medicine, minerals and fossils.
- Added three group encounters: common swarm, rare duo and starter parade.
- Added the Crystal Altar and Healing Garden.
- Added four non-lethal unlucky events: thunder scare, sticky webs, hunger and fake gold.
- Lucky tuning now supports every block variant and preserves its type.
- Added full Brazilian Portuguese and English text for all new content.
- Added automated resource-integrity tests for models, textures, recipes and translations.

Cobblemon Raid Dens remains optional. Without it, Raid Den outcomes safely become rare Pokémon encounters.
